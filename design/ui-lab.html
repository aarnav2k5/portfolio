About the Developer

§ 1.0  /  General

Full-stack developer who builds seamless web apps — from intuitive interfaces to scalable back-ends. The work spans the whole span of a product: the schema that holds it up, the API that moves it, and the interface a person actually touches.

In practice that means React and Next.js on the surface, Node.js and Express beneath it, and Postgres, MySQL or Supabase underneath that.[2] Currently building ERP systems at ZenDevX, where the client is on the call and the requirements change on Tuesday.

This document catalogues six shipped projects, two in development, one role, twelve instruments, and the means of correspondence. It is meant to be read, not skimmed.
